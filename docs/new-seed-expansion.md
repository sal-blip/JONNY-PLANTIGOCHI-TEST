# Twenty new growable plants

The original roster contains nine growable species. This first proposal describes twenty possible additions. The three carnivorous plants shipped alongside the separate twenty-species collection below. These are free seed choices after graduation and use the same earned-growth clock and care actions. The shop keeps its separate fully grown collection. The first Monstera and outstanding seed offers keep their identities.

| 6 | Black Bat Flower · Passionflower (Passiflora caerulea) · Flaming Torch Bromeliad (Billbergia pyramidalis) | Dark bracts and whiskers · a ring of filaments · a red inflorescence with violet tips |

Passes 2–7 are proposed. All proposed species can use the existing pot system.

Each pass needs five keychain stages, idle keyframes, thirsty posture, seed packet copy and a mature SpriteCook crown. Check the silhouette in iSEED and the soil anchor across the four garden pots plus a shelf view. Use the succession and save round-trip test before release. The first three species use the normal graduation and placement flow.


## Additional playable collection

The carnivorous trio has passed its first art review. Twenty further species, distinct from the seventeen proposed above, join them in version 0.1.31. This makes 32 playable species including the original nine. All twenty use the existing two-packet offer, shared care clock, mature inventory handoff and iSEED portrait unlock.

The source of names, packet hints and day-five reveals is [collectorSeeds.json](../src/collectorSeeds.json). The keychain atlas is authored independently at the toy’s native scale. Each atlas contains five stages and three idle keyframes per stage. The garden uses the SpriteCook crowns on the standard four-layer pot renderer. Per-specimen corrections include mounting the Bird’s Nest Fern at its central rosette while allowing the low frond to hang over the rim.
