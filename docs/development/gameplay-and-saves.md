Plant and pot IDs resolve independently at the current slot. On a move the transaction checks the selected pair and destination against the latest save. The [garden composition specification](interface-and-art.md#pot-and-plant-composition) defines the visual result for carrying, swapping, repotting and reload.



## Growth and care

Start with `game.js` when changing a rule. Follow a water button press from the keychain into `care(saved, 'water', now, confirmed)`. It calls `wake` to resolve elapsed time, checks the same `careStatus` used by the menu, settles earned growth, then updates the plant. Blocked actions return the current state after waking. The LCD derives its meters from that state, so a change to watering eligibility should flow through to its readout. [Game rules](../../src/game.js) · [Button handlers](../../src/PocketDevice.jsx) · [LCD readouts](../../src/plantReadout.js)

| Rule | Where to change it | Behavior to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Growth | `earnedAt`, `wake`, `nextMilestone`, `milestoneDue` | A watered seed sprouts on a visible wake at or after one hour from planting. Later milestones use **earned** time at days 3, 5 and 7. `earned` and `anchor` record settled progress. Critical thirst, overdue food or light, root rot and active fungus stop accrual. |
| Soil and food | `condition`, `careStatus`, `care`, `soilDeadline` | After sprouting, soil dries at 4 hours, becomes critical at 12, and reaches its thirst deadline at 24. A confirmed extra pour can soak the soil. Another confirmed pour at least an hour later in the same wet period can cause rot. Food and light have 48- and 24-hour intervals and each opens 8 hours early. |
| Illness and death | `discoverFungus`, `treatFungus`, `deathDeadline`, `wake` | Fungus becomes eligible 36–60 hours after sprouting or treatment. Seeing it on the eligible main toy screen starts the 12-hour rescue window. Treatment checks the outbreak timestamp. Root rot has its own 12-hour rescue window. A missed deadline records one pressed leaf and its cause. |
| Company and succession | `company`, `moodOf`, `graduate`, `replant` | Talk and completed game rounds update company. Mood is independent of physical growth. Graduation moves the active mature plant into a garden slot and saves two free seed offers. Choosing a species starts its clock and opens naming. Snake Plant remains supported for legacy gardens. A rooted 12-hour cutting starts a new generation of the parent’s variety after death. |

The LCD uses the Care menu’s eligibility rules. Concurrent needs appear in one compact row above the meters, ordered water, sunlight, food and company. Each square fades independently as its need changes. The LCD dims when sunlight opens at 16 hours and deepens at 24 hours. First Aid and root rot take precedence. Use explicit millisecond timestamps in tests. Check the exact boundary, a reload after time away, and an action timestamp older than the last recorded care. The visible game refreshes the display, while timestamps let the simulation catch up when the app reopens. Review the reminder schedule when a change moves a care or growth deadline. [Simulation timing tests](../../tests/simulationTiming.test.js) · [Growth tests](../../tests/growth.test.js) · [Fungus tests](../../tests/fungus.test.js) · [Reminder schedule](../../src/reminderSchedule.js)

The active plant, cuttings and pressed leaves record their variety independently of the player’s chosen name. Records that predate the variety field read as Monstera. Earlier active plants continue their current cycle and use the new order when they move in. The day-five milestone is called **Vining plant** for Golden Pothos, **Rosette** for Snake Plant, **First runner** for Hoya Splash, **First trail** for Monkey Tail Cactus followed by **Velvet blade** for Queen Anthurium, **Blush vine** for Pink Micans, **Flower bract** for Bird of Paradise and **Budding spike** for Moth Orchid. Adding another variety to the order also requires its LCD art across growth stages and its garden sprite. [Lifecycle and save validation](../../src/game.js) · [LCD plant art](../../src/PlantPixel.jsx) · [Succession checks](../../tests/collector.test.js)

Each newly earned stage queues a radial flare for the next visible keychain visit. At maturity, the save also records the plant in `waitingPlants`. iSEED Inventory can take that same earned plant into room placement after a cancellation or reload. Confirming an open spot removes the inventory entry, places the original plant and saves the pair of seed offers in one transaction. If the active plant has already been replaced, placing an older earned plant only updates the collection. Each successor carries its own pending naming prompt until the player names it or keeps the default on the keychain. ## Testing growth on a phone

In Settings, hold both lower corners for five seconds. Choose **Growth Lab → Start test copy** to run the normal toy and garden against a disposable copy held in memory. The lab badge reopens its clock controls. One real minute advances three plant hours. Jump and fast-forward stop at care, illness and growth events. Water, feed, treat and move the plant through the usual controls. Skip an individual reminder to test neglect. The last rescue warning requires its own skip before time can reach death. Hiding the app pauses fast-forward. Exiting the lab or reloading discards the copy.

The calendar for rewards and the room lighting follows the device. Production reminders always use the saved garden. **1,000 coins in purse** sets an exact balance after you confirm. In the ordinary debug menu it changes the saved purse. Inside Growth Lab it changes the test copy. [Lab clock and stops](../../src/growthLab.js) · [Regression tests](../../tests/growthLab.test.js)

## Games and rewards

`stepGame(state, event)` runs a round from key and tick events. `dewRules(level)` controls droplet speed, spacing and target. All three games are available from seed. Dew Catch shares three strikes across 20 levels. A loss resets the run to level one while preserving the personal best. Vine Trail advances one cell per tick with one queued relative turn. Collect five suns to win. Leaf Pairs shuffles six cards at round start and holds a mismatch briefly before hiding it. The opening water lesson offers optional play and resumes on exit. The existing tutorial wallet and regular daily reward rules govern earnings. To change game balance, drive the reducer with explicit events in a test. Follow `recordRound` for the saved checkpoint, win receipt and company update. [Game reducer](../../src/pocketPlay.js) · [Result handler](../../src/PocketDevice.jsx) · [Game and survival tests](../../tests/survival.test.js)

The toy’s Growth Spurt entry uses the same offer calculation as the purchase transaction. Each 50-coin spurt adds a quarter of the current stage, capped at its next milestone. The first sprout uses four purchases after a one-time 200-coin gift. Its final debit and the start of normal care share a transaction. The sprout greeting then offers one optional round of Dew Catch. The save keeps the remaining chances and caught drops so the player can resume. The first win, third miss, or decision to skip leads to a farewell on the toy. Claiming the 20-coin gift credits the wallet and clears the pending farewell together. Game winnings are separate. Older saves in the app tour resume at the garden return gesture. Older saves in the final toy tour enter normal care from the migration time. Confirmation rechecks funds, care, the expected stage and purchase count against the latest save. See `growthSpurtOffer` and `buyGrowth` in [the simulation](../../src/game.js).

The wallet, purchases, journal events, real-plant records and saved notes live in `gardenLife`. The first visible visit opens a dated receipt. Claiming credits the seven-day ladder. A second action draws one free spin. Keep the result and payout in the same IndexedDB transaction. The iSEED badge counts both outstanding actions and closing the reveal leaves them available for the rest of the local day. The reward repeats after day seven. Legacy wallets treat their last paid visit as collected. A day left unclaimed breaks the streak. Tutorial watering and the 12-coin pot retain their existing economy. Eligible watering earns 2 coins up to three times per day. A win earns 3 coins up to five times per day, with a unique receipt and matching start and finish dates. Keep spending and placement together in `placeItem`, which rechecks the destination and balance before charging. Journal changes compare the original entry when editing or deleting it. Unsaved drafts live in the current garden session. ## Saving an edit

Garden plants and purchased pots have independent IDs and share a slot while potted. Moving with a purchased pot sends a displaced destination pot to inventory. Repotting moves only the plant into an empty player-placed pot and verifies the destination against the latest save. Moving a pot under an existing plant preserves that plant's slot. If moving the last resident closes a distant room, its stranded items go to inventory in the same transaction. [Placement rules](../../src/gardenEconomy.js) · [Save regressions](../../tests/collector.test.js)

Call `commit(current => next)` from the UI. The hook queues operations, and the store reads the latest plant inside a read/write IndexedDB transaction. Base the change on that argument and return it unchanged if the action is no longer possible. If the UI promises that a purchase or move happened, check both whether the operation changed the plant and whether the transaction succeeded. A successful no-op can occur when another window already moved the item. [Save hook](../../src/useGardenSave.js) · [Transactions](../../src/gardenStore.js) · [Room operations](../../src/Collector.jsx)

The database is `plantigochi.pocket.garden`, object store `garden`. The `current` record holds plant JSON. The `setup` record holds onboarding phase, epoch and revision. A replacement or reset rotates the epoch. A pending destructive confirmation also checks the reviewed content to detect edits from older windows. Reset commits the erase and a cleanup marker together so interrupted preference and alert cleanup can resume. Reminder work for an enrolled device is staged in the plant transaction. [Store](../../src/gardenStore.js) · [Conflict and recovery tests](../../tests/gardenStore.test.js) · [Reminder transaction tests](../../tests/reminderOutbox.test.js)

## Changing the save format

1. Define the new field’s initial value in `newPlant` or the `gardenLife` defaults. Decide whether it belongs to the active plant or follows the collection through `graduate` and `replant`. Both functions merge a fresh seed over the previous garden. [Lifecycle](../../src/game.js) · [Economy defaults](../../src/gardenEconomy.js)
2. Update `readPlant` and the appropriate validator. It migrates v1 and v2 plant records to v3 and supplies missing fields for supported older saves. Check old records, present-but-invalid values, and repeated reads. An unreadable or unsupported save goes to recovery, where the original can be exported. [Save reader](../../src/game.js) · [Recovery screen](../../src/Recovery.jsx)
3. Round-trip through the actual backup path. Gardens without available frame originals export JSON. A `.plantigochi` bundle carries the garden and original image bytes. Frame previews remain in JSON and originals are separate IndexedDB blobs. A frame upload commits both together, and a restore assigns fresh original IDs. When adding media, exercise replacement, deletion, insufficient space and interrupted restore. [Backup codec](../../src/gardenBackup.js) · [Storage tests](../../tests/gardenStore.test.js)

## Worked example

To open safe watering **45 minutes before dry soil**, change `WATER_TOP_UP_WINDOW` in `game.js` from half an hour to 45 minutes. The existing four-hour interval would open the top-up at 3h 15m after the last drink. Update the half-hour case in the survival tests to check one millisecond before opening, the opening instant, the meter text and the next watering deadline after a successful top-up. Keep the normal dry and critical thresholds in place, and update the player-facing timing in the README. ### Garden spin

The daily table in [the economy module](../../src/gardenEconomy.js) drives both the draw and the odds shown in the dialog. The free spin goes first. An additional spin costs one token, which costs 100 Garden Coins. The wallet stores lifetime purchase and spend counts, so stale requests cannot debit twice. The daily badge counts the free spin and coin claim. A paid spin keeps its latest prize in the visit record, credits any winnings, and debits its token in the same transaction. Tokens carry across dates. One spin awards 10 coins (35%), 25 (25%), 50 (15%), 100 (5%), a day of growth (10%), a shop pot (7%) or a grown shop plant (3%). Each eligible SKU is equally likely within its prize category. Pots enter loose inventory. Plants enter waiting inventory with shop provenance and can be placed while the current seed continues growing. A growth day stays in the garden wallet until used on a healthy sprouted plant. It advances earned growth by up to 24 hours, capped at maturity, and preserves care deadlines. The result survives reopening the app during the reel animation. The SpriteCook [art record](../art-review/daily-spin/spritecook-assets.json) includes the reel aperture and source assets.
