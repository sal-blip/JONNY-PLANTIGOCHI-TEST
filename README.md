# Plantigochi

Raise a plant in a leaf-shaped pocket keychain, then give it a permanent spot in your pixel-art garden. Your first seed is a Monstera. When you bring a fully grown plant home, it stays in the room without needing care, and a new species takes its turn on the keychain. The garden follows the time of day on your phone.

[Open Plantigochi](https://plantigochi-pocket-garden-mobile.salbasilone.chatgpt.site/) · [Developer guide](docs/development/README.md)

## Install and grow your first sprout

Open the game link on your phone. The welcome tour previews Vine Trail, Dew Catch, and Leaf Pairs before it ends with **Install app**. On Android, accept Chrome’s install prompt when offered. On iPhone, open the link in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen → Add**. If you came from Facebook, follow the handoff to your phone’s browser. Open the new Home Screen icon to play. If the browser already holds a garden, the link shows **Welcome back, gardener** with help opening or transferring it.

1. **Name your seed and give it a drink.** The leaf case has four buttons. Heart opens a menu or selects an item, the arrows browse, and Back goes up one level. Follow the chat to **Care → Water**. Your first visit earns 10 Garden Coins and the first watering adds 2.
2. **Find a home for it.** Visit the garden, tap the iSEED phone at the lower right, and open **Shop**. Tap the terracotta pot to see it up close, choose **Buy**, then tap a glowing spot and confirm. The tutorial pot costs exactly the 12 coins you just earned.
3. **See it sprout.** Back at the keychain, you receive 200 coins for the lesson. Buy four 50-coin spurts under **Growth → Growth Spurt**. The fourth reveals the Monstera and starts its regular care clock.
4. **Finish the lesson.** Choose **Play Vine Trail** or **Maybe later** when your Monstera sprouts. After your run, the toy tells you where to find Dew Catch and Leaf Pairs. Either path lets you claim 20 good-luck coins. On supported devices, the plant can then offer care reminders. You can revisit that choice under **App & data → Plant alerts**.

## Care for the plant on the keychain

Read the growth, water and mood meters under the plant. The badges just above them show which needs are open, and the screen edges darken when the plant could use light. Use the case buttons to give care. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys, Enter and Escape.

| Need | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Water | Once sprouted, the plant gets thirsty four hours after a drink. A normal top-up opens 30 minutes early. At 12 hours the plant is critically thirsty, and at 24 hours without water it dies. A thirsty plant wilts. Repeated extra pours into wet soil can cause root rot, which gives you 12 hours to drain the pot. |
| Light and food | Sunlight is due every 24 hours and food every 48 hours. Each opens eight hours early. Overdue light or food pauses growth, as do critical thirst, root rot and active fungus. |
| Company | Kind words or finishing a game lift the plant’s mood. After 12 hours without company it grows quiet, and after 24 hours it gets lonely. |
| Fungus gnats | When you first see an outbreak on the keychain, a 12-hour rescue window opens. Use the G-NAT ASSASSIN dropper under **First Aid** to clear it. |

After the tutorial, a new seed needs water and one hour of growth to sprout naturally. Time away counts. With steady care, the young stage arrives at three days of earned growth, a species-specific stage at five, and full growth at seven. **Growth Spurt** can spend 50 coins to advance up to a quarter of the current stage when care needs are met. **Growth → Rename plant** lets you change the name that follows it into the garden.

After naming your seed, press the heart and follow the prompts for its first drink. All three games are in the toy’s **Play** menu. In **Vine Trail**, steer the growing tip toward suns with Left and Right. The fifth sun earns the usual game reward. Keep collecting until you hit an edge, hit your vine, or fill the board. **Dew Catch** has 20 levels of falling drops. Three misses reset the run to level one. In **Leaf Pairs**, use the arrows to select one of eighteen packets and Heart to flip it. Find all nine matching pairs before your fifth miss. Press Heart after a loss to shuffle and try again. Tap Back to pause a game or hold it to return to the plant. During regular play, daily visits earn 10 to 30 coins on a seven-day streak, qualifying waterings earn 2 each up to three times a day, and game wins earn 3 each up to five times a day.

## Bring a grown plant home

At full growth, your plant appears in **iSEED → Inventory**. Tap **Choose a garden spot** on the keychain, or resume from Inventory, then select a spot and confirm. Placing the active plant saves two free seed packets. On your keychain, browse with the arrows, choose with Heart, then name your seed. Back takes you to the garden and keeps both offers for later. The first pair after Monstera is Prayer Plant and Monkey Tail Cactus. Later pairs favor species you haven’t raised. There are 32 growable species. You can browse their names and silhouettes in the iSEED Seed collection. The Prayer Plant spreads its red-veined leaves by day and lifts them at night.

Swipe up from the keychain to visit the garden. Swipe up or down on open scenery to return, or tap the bottom chevrons on either screen. Move sideways between the living room and studio. Each has twelve plant spots, and more rooms open as the collection grows. Tap open scenery for a five-second closer look. The cutting jar on the living-room shelf can root a cutting from a fully grown active plant over 12 hours. The Leaf Book on the studio desk keeps a pressed leaf when a plant dies. You can then start again with a seed or pot a rooted cutting.

Tap a garden object to inspect it. If a plant occupies a purchased pot, choose what you want to handle before choosing a destination.

- **Plant only** repots the plant into an available empty pot. The original purchased pot stays in place. Empty shelves are open for placing a plant or pot.
- **Pot only** lets you move, swap or store the container. The plant stays in its spot.
- **Plant + pot** moves both together. Choose a pulsing placement circle and confirm. Rearranging the room is free.

## Shop and use iSEED

Tap the phone icon in the garden. The seed-shaped Home button returns to the iSEED launcher. Swipe down from the bezel or tap **Garden** to put the phone away.

| App | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Shop** | Buy pots, photo frames, decorations or fully grown plants with Garden Coins. **Compare garden sizes** shows each item beside a terracotta pot at windowsill depth. Tap an item for a closer look. Back returns to browsing. Buy lets you choose a spot, and confirming placement spends the coins. |
| **Plantigotchi** | See your current plant’s growth and care readings, read the toy guide, and browse all 32 named silhouettes. Each species you raise to maturity reveals its full-color portrait. |
| **Inventory** | Find a mature plant awaiting placement, place stored items, or manage items already in the room. |
| **Journal** | Read your pocket plant’s milestones, write notes, and track observations, watering, feeding and repotting for your real plants. |
| **Daily rewards** | On your first visit each day, claim your coins and take a free Garden spin. The button beside the iSEED menu shows how many actions are waiting. Seven consecutive claims pay 10, 10, 15, 15, 20, 20 and 30 coins. The next week starts over. Spin prizes include coins, an inventory pot, a grown shop plant or a banked day of growth. Extra spins use one token each. Tap **Buy token** or your token balance to buy one for 100 Garden Coins. The daily free spin goes first. Open **Prizes & odds** to see every chance. |
| **Journal → Care library** | Read built-in plant guides offline or look up real species online. The last six opened species guides are available offline. |
| **Share** | Choose a room, tap a plant to focus, drag to frame it and adjust zoom from 1× to 4×. Take a photo, then retake, save the PNG or share it on supported devices. |

The regular terracotta pot costs **10 coins** after the tutorial. Six more garden pots are available in iSEED: Honey-Glazed Terracotta (16), Low Sandstone Bowl (20), Rain Boot (24), Blue Willow Porcelain (28), Stump Cottage (34) and Ringed Planet (38). The nine fully grown shop plants have a separate roster: Snake Plant (100 coins), Pearls and Jade Pothos (150), Raven ZZ (180), Polka Dot Begonia (225), Pink Princess Philodendron (375), Monstera Albo (450), Thai Constellation (550), Variegated Billie (650) and Homalomena Pink Diamond (700). Existing gardens keep plants bought under earlier catalogs. Buying a grown plant places it directly in the room while your keychain plant continues its own cycle. ## Saves, settings and reminders

Progress saves on this device. To move a garden or keep a copy before uninstalling, open the three-wave menu and choose **App & data → Saved plant → Back up**. On a fresh installation choose **Restore a garden** during setup. The backup includes the plant, purse, room placements, journal and saved frame photos. In iSEED → Share, drag to frame your garden and use the right-hand slider to zoom. Tap the center shutter to add a photo to this device’s camera roll. Open the lower-left thumbnail to browse or save photos. The lower-right button shares the selected or latest photo. Save photos separately to keep them when moving your garden. Use the backup file to restore playable progress.

Once the game finishes caching, you can reopen it offline. Care reminders require permission and a server-confirmed schedule. Water reminders are due at four and twelve hours after a drink. Delivery also depends on the dispatch queue, connectivity and the phone’s notification settings. On iPhone and iPad, Web Push requires a Home Screen installation on iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later.

The three-wave menu also lets you change backgrounds or use your own photo. **Settings** has separate music and sound effects sliders, tilt controls, update checks and uninstall help. Music and sound effects start at 40%. The optional garden record player plays Jason Singh’s orchid-biodata recording *Earth Day*. ## Work on the game

The client uses React and Canvas 2D. IndexedDB holds the garden. The browser service worker caches the game and displays push notifications. A separate hosted Worker uses D1 for reminder delivery.

Use Node.js 24. Start the development server from the repository root.

```sh
npm ci
npm run dev
```

The dev server opens the public install tour. Run `npm test` for the unit suite and `npm run build` to produce the client and hosted Worker in `dist/`. For installed-app and offline checks, serve that build with `npx vite preview --host 127.0.0.1`. For a fresh tutorial playtest on a device, back up your garden, open **Settings**, hold one finger in each lower corner for five seconds, and choose **Debug menu → Start a fresh game**.
