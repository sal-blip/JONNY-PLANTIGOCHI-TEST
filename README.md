# JONNY-PLANTIGOCHI-TEST

Plantigochi is a small React game about raising a cheeky Monstera in a leaf-shaped pocket keychain. The first seed starts sleeping. It sprouts when an hour has elapsed since planting and it has received water. The timer includes time spent with the browser closed.

Under steady care the Monstera grows from seed through Sprout, Young (day 3), First Split (day 5) and Grown (day 7). Critical thirst, rot, overdue food or overdue light pause earned growing time. Feeding recurs every 48 hours and sunlight every 24 hours. Each becomes available eight hours early. Kind words or completing a game give 30 minutes of happiness. Content plants become quiet after 12 hours without company and lonely after 24 hours. Press the heart to open the plant menu. Your Monstera stays centered while one item and its short description appear underneath. The arrows move the highlight, the heart selects, and the hooked-arrow Back key returns to the previous selection. The LCD is display-only. The browser saves progress and sound preferences.

The public link introduces the game and guides installation. Gameplay opens from the installed app icon. On a new installation, choose **Name your seed** or **Restore a garden**. Confirming a name starts the seed clock. The first lesson uses the real toy buttons to open **Care → Water**. Setup resumes after reopening. Existing gardens open directly.

Choose **Growth → Rename plant** on the toy to edit the current plant’s name. The arrows browse letters and commands, Heart picks, Back erases, and holding Back cancels. Select **Done** to save. Names follow plants into the garden and the Leaf Book.

For a fresh playtest, choose **Settings → Reset all Plantigochi data**. Review the data, confirm the installation, then type **RESET** and choose **Delete everything and restart**. The installed app replays all four welcome cards and offers **Begin setup**. The reset covers this installation’s garden, history, photo, preferences and alerts. A downloaded backup can be restored from the welcome screen. Offline reset silences alerts locally and queues server cancellation until the connection returns.

Tap the three waves at the top right to open the glass pocket menu. The glass menu contains backgrounds, settings, and app installation, alerts and saves. Care, Play, Growth, First Aid and the Field guide are on the toy LCD. Tap the cutting jar on the living-room wall shelf or the Leaf Book on the studio desk to open it. Choose a scene under **Backgrounds**. Use **Use My Photo** to select a local image and drag it into place. A backup includes the resized photo and its framing as well as your plant. New players see the four welcome cards during setup. Fresh gardens start with sound enabled. The game plays at launch when the browser permits it, or on the first tap or keypress. Existing mute preferences are respected. **Settings** controls sound, the continuous synth and sleeping-seed ambience. Reduced-motion preferences skip the automatic leaf reveal.

Press a toy key to ease closer to the dot-matrix screen. Menus and games hold the close-up. After returning to the idle screen, the camera eases back. The LCD uses the bundled Silkscreen font under the SIL Open Font License.

A sprouted Monstera dries out after 4 hours, becomes critical at 12, and dies after 24 without a drink. From 3 hours 30 minutes, a normal pour on healthy soil safely refills the water meter and restarts the clock. Dry and critically thirsty plants keep their leaves and look sad until watered. Two confirmed extra pours at least an hour apart in the same wet period cause root rot. Drain the pot within 12 hours. Care shows each need in place. Sleeping seeds are safe. After sprouting or treatment, a fungus outbreak becomes possible in 36–60 hours. The 12-hour rescue clock starts when you first see the gnats on the main toy screen. Growth pauses until you use one drop of G-NAT ASSASSIN under **First Aid**. Garden graduates are safe. A death adds a pressed leaf with its age, generation, and cause to the Leaf Book.

Open **Play** on the LCD for Dew Catch as a sprout and Leaf Echo as a young plant. Dew Catch has 20 levels. The target rises from three to 22 catches, the drops speed up and later levels have several in flight. Your current checkpoint and personal best carry over in backups and across generations. Each Dew Catch run starts with three droplet lives shared across levels. On the third miss, the game briefly shows the result and automatically restarts at level 1 with three fresh lives. Tap Back to pause either game. Hold Back for 0.7 seconds to return directly to your Monstera. Holding Escape works the same way on a keyboard. Keyboard controls include the arrow keys, Enter and Escape. A grown Monstera can root one cutting in a jar over 12 real hours. The jar survives the parent and a rooted cutting can start the next generation. The local save and exported backup include the jar and keepsakes.

The chain hangs from a single eyelet. Grab inside the ring or along an exposed link. Flick outward past the leaf edge to swing the ring behind it, or hold it still before lifting to let it go gently. Tap the eyelet to bring it back. On supported phones, the links follow gravity as you turn the device. A moving highlight and soft shadow follow the leaf surface. The background has a light blur. On supported phones, tilting moves the scenery up to 17 pixels and the keychain up to 5 pixels in the opposite direction, with a one-degree swing. **Settings → Tilt motion** controls the effect and requests sensor access on phones that require it. Holding the phone naturally when returning to the garden sets the center. Tilt respects the device’s reduced-motion preference.

Stonewashed Pocket uses a [denim photograph](https://www.pexels.com/photo/photo-of-denim-pants-6843233/) under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Raked Sand, Still Water, Cotton Clouds, Shoji Morning and Pebble Garden use original artwork.

## Living-room garden

Swipe up on the Garden tab or tap it. The collector opens onto a tall pot shelf in the living room. Swipe left to visit the adjoining plant studio, or use the room arrows. At full growth, choose **Growth → Move to garden**, select a spot and confirm. The graduate and new sleeping seed save together. A graduate can move later. Tap it and then an empty spot, or drag it to a shelf, windowsill, coffee table, desk or floor. Hold a dragged plant at an edge to travel to the adjoining view. Cancel returns it to its saved place.

Each two-view apartment has twelve display spots. Filling it opens another pair of views. Slot numbers and graduate IDs survive reloads and backup transfers. The current species is Monstera. The room uses live Canvas 2D game graphics, with its own scene objects, layered plant and cat sprites, and phone-local daylight. The postcard captures those game objects at their saved positions as a shareable PNG.

## Garden shop and journal

Tap **iSEED**, the phone on the living room coffee table or studio desk. Its home screen opens **Shop**, **Inventory**, **Journal**, **Care library**, and **Garden postcard**. The three-wave button opens the same game settings in the garden and at the keychain. The first gameplay visit each local day earns 10 Garden Coins. Successful seed watering, safe top-ups and watering a thirsty plant earn 2 coins up to three times a day. Winning a Dew Catch level or Leaf Echo round earns 3 coins up to five times a day. The wallet, room items and journal travel with your garden backup.

Choose an item, tap a placement circle and confirm the purchase. A 10-coin terracotta pot is affordable on day one. Bought plants arrive grown. Each purchased pot or decoration is a physical item that can move around the room. Frames accept a personal photo, and the record player includes Jason Singh’s orchid-biodata recording *Earth Day* for offline listening. [Recording and attribution](public/records/earth-day-credits.txt)

To rearrange the room, tap a decoration or pot, choose **Move to another spot**, and select a marked surface. Use the room arrows to move across rooms. Confirm with **Place here** or **Swap here**. Rearranging costs no coins. Tapping foliage moves the plant with its pot. Tapping a purchased pot underneath moves the pot independently.

**My Plantigochi** combines milestones and notes. **My real plants** keeps personal observations and care logs by plant. **Care library** includes offline guides and searches English Wikipedia for credited cultivation and propagation sections. The last six opened species guides are available offline on that device.

## Run locally

Use Node.js 22 or newer.

```sh
npm ci
npm run dev
```

Run `npm test` to verify watering, elapsed time, saved progress, and all five growth stages. Use `npm run build` for a static production build in `dist/`.

## Project layout

`src/game.js` contains the growth rules and save format. `src/App.jsx` handles the interactive keychain and care panel. The original plant sprites and leaf shell live in `src/PlantPixel.jsx` and `public/art/device.svg`.

## Install on a phone

Open the published Site online and choose **Get Plantigochi**. If you played an earlier version in the browser, the install card offers **Export existing garden** so you can transfer that save when the phone uses separate storage. Confirm the browser’s install prompt when offered. On iPhone, open the game in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen → Add**. On Android, Chrome also offers installation from its ⋮ menu. The install screen shows **Ready for offline play** once the game is cached.

Progress is stored in IndexedDB after each care action. Valid older saves migrate on first open. An unreadable or newer-format save opens recovery with an option to export the original. **App & saves → Saved plant → Back up** exports a save that can be restored on a different device or in a newly installed copy. Moving to a different Site origin creates separate browser storage. For a hosting move also set the canonical URL and Open Graph URLs in index.html to the new public origin.

The installed app checks for new releases when it opens or returns to the foreground online. A downloaded update can activate while another Plantigochi window stays open. Reopen the app to load it, or choose **Settings → Check for updates** and then **Restart to update** when offered. Updates use the existing local garden.

The production build uses vite-plugin-pwa and Workbox to generate a manifest and a versioned service worker that caches the game, artwork, icons and fonts. Service workers require HTTPS (or localhost for development). The normal Vite dev server does not register the production worker.

## Plant alerts

The installed welcome offers **Turn on plant alerts** before naming. The phone asks for permission when the player taps that button. **Maybe later** defers the invitation to the next installed launch, where **Not now** finishes it. A full garden reset restarts this sequence. Players can also change their choice under **App & saves → Plant alerts**. Enrollment works before a seed exists. The first watering schedules the sprout reminder. On iPhone and iPad, Web Push requires iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later. Open the game from its Home Screen icon and tap **Enable plant alerts**. Focus and Lock Screen notification settings control how iOS presents the alerts. The game schedules reachable growth milestones, critical rescue reminders, and optional company nudges after ten hours. Rescue takes priority. Only the active plant can create reminders. Water alerts are due at 4 hours and again at 12 hours without a drink. Tapping a current water alert opens Care at Water. An alert from a previous watering cycle opens the plant screen. Each successful watering replaces the outstanding reminders. Root-rot and fungus reminders are due six hours into their rescue windows. The hosted scheduler checks the queue hourly, so delivery can be up to one additional hour later. Growth transitions also notify an opted-in player. Phones control delivery timing, permissions, and quiet modes.

In **Settings**, hold one finger inside each lower corner of the glass pane for five seconds to open the hidden **Debug menu**. Choose **Test notifications**, enable plant alerts if needed and tap **Send two test alerts**. The server sends the first push after five seconds and the second after fifteen. Keep the app open for the first, then close it and lock the phone. Reopen the debug test to see each result. “Sent to push service” records provider acceptance. “Received on this device” records that the service worker displayed the notification.

The Site Worker stores device push subscriptions, outstanding reminders, short-lived delivery records, the most recent test status and a shared, expiring Apple sender token in D1. Game progress stays on the player's device. Provide a stable P-256 VAPID key pair through runtime variables `PUSH_PUBLIC_KEY` (raw base64url) and `PUSH_PRIVATE_JWK` (secret). The build packages the Worker and the generated Drizzle migrations. The first migration creates the reminder queue. To generate future migrations after schema changes, run `npx drizzle-kit generate`.

### Scheduled delivery

The production scheduler posts an empty request to `/api/alerts/dispatch`. It is safe to repeat. Only existing, player-authorized reminders whose delivery time has passed can be claimed, and the caller cannot select a recipient, message, or delivery time. The result contains aggregate counts. A batch handles up to 25 reminders; a scheduler can repeat if a full batch was returned. No subscription endpoints or keys belong in logs or task instructions.
