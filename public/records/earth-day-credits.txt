Jason Singh composed this soundscape from orchid biodata in the UK and
field recordings contributed from around Swarthmore, Pennsylvania, and
Puerto Rico during a residency with Swarthmore College.

The Plantigochi edition contains the complete recording. The artist's
Bandcamp download was re-encoded as 24 kHz stereo AAC for offline
playback. The duration and sequence are unchanged.
